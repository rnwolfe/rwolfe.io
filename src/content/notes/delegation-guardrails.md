title: "Delegate constraints, not solutions"
pubDate: 2026-02-03
type: til
tags: ["delegation", "productivity", "engineering"]
---

A surprisingly good default:

- Don’t delegate *how*.
- Delegate *constraints*.

Example constraints that actually protect quality:
- backwards compatible
- observable
- easy rollback
- clear definition of done

It’s the difference between guidance and remote-control engineering.
