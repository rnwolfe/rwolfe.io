This is a useful reminder that operators are not background plumbing, they are active embodiments of application logic. Because they mediate scaling, upgrades, failover, and recovery, operator metrics can be early warning signals for application trouble. It made me think that operator observability should be treated as first-class production monitoring, not something tucked behind app dashboards.
