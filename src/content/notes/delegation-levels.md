---
title: "Delegation has levels (and mismatching them creates pain)"
pubDate: 2026-02-03
type: thought
tags: ["delegation", "leadership"]
---

When someone says “delegate,” they might mean three different things:

1) **Do exactly this** (recipe execution)
2) **Achieve this outcome** (you propose a plan; I review risk/constraints)
3) **Own this area** (direction + boundaries; you drive)

Most delegation pain is a mismatch:
- expecting level 3 behavior with level 1 authority
- or delegating level 3 responsibility with level 1 clarity
