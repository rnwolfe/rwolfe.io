---
title: "Make delegation check-ins about risk, not status"
pubDate: 2026-02-03
type: thought
tags: ["leadership", "delegation", "process"]
---

Check-ins shouldn’t be “are you done yet?”

Make them *risk gates*:
- approach chosen
- unknowns resolved
- PR drafted
- rollout plan + rollback ready
- metrics/alerts in place

You stay out of the weeds without flying blind.
